# Kuymo — concept brief

Name approved by the owner: **Kuymo**, 2026-11-25. The core brand uses no AI
suffix. **Kuymo AI** may identify an assistant context later; it is not a second
app name or a separate brand. The name is coined; no literal Japanese meaning
or kanji is claimed.

Promise: prepare videos to share with less effort, predictable size limits and
on-device processing. Personality: warm, capable, simple and attentive.

## Concept longlist

1. Fold K: a soft, folded letter K opens into an onward diagonal.
2. A little room: an open pocket makes space for one small independent tile.
3. Pocket companion: a compact rounded creature with a pocket-shaped smile.
4. Paired brackets make a calm protective frame around a video.
5. A folded corner becomes a Kuymo K inside a rounded square.
6. A small stone and its ripple suggest a lighter result with lasting impact.
7. Five modular rounded tiles turn the letters of Kuymo into a pattern.
8. An origami wing folds inward to occupy less space.
9. A ribbon loop links the original file and the smaller result.
10. A forward step cut into a lowercase k suggests easy progress.

Build the first three to compare letterform, abstract and mascot approaches.
The final recommendation after refinement is A, **Fold K**: the letter reads
immediately, while the returning lower arm creates a pocket/tail detail that
can become part of the visual system. Its risk is resemblance to other K-led
brands, requiring further distinction checks before release. B expresses making
room, but can read as a utility/storage product or the letter C. C is warmest
but has more generic mascot associations and less technical precision.

## Construction

- All symbols use a 256 square, a predominantly 8-unit grid, solid black fills
  and open negative space. No filters, embedded raster, or live text.
- A uses a 56-unit rounded stem, a 45-degree upper branch, and a lower branch
  returning upward into a pocket. Pass 2 centers the silhouette horizontally.
- B uses 64-unit outer/24-unit inner pocket radii and a separate 56-unit tile
  with a folded/chamfered lower corner in pass 2.
- C uses a broad organic silhouette, rounder ears, 28-unit eye openings and
  an open smile after pass 2.
- Lockups are an outlined Google Sans Bold type study from the font already in
  the repository, with exploratory spacing; custom typography is not final.

This directory is for **concept selection**, not an approved identity kit.
No color, launcher, splash, mascot-state or UI-theme rollout is implied by
these drawings. After selection, refine and build the chosen system.
