# Kuymo — Ethical choice architecture

Behavior: understand the product → explore current screenshots → optionally follow releases before Play publication. The task is simple and the visitor may have low readiness. Remove uncertainty before asking for commitment.

Scores are qualitative hypotheses, not conversion evidence. PLFS = Leverage + Fit + Speed + Ethics − Implementation Cost, capped at 15, using 1–5 per factor.

## Mental model: reduce choice overload

**PLFS: +15** (5 + 5 + 4 + 5 − 2 = 17, capped).

- Why: a primary route lowers the effort of deciding where to start.
- Behavior: first-time visitor explores the app.
- Where: hero and floating navigation.
- Implementation: primary screenshot action; secondary feature route; release-following is visible but distinct. No pricing tiers or sign-up detour.
- Test: ask a visitor what the main action does before they click; observe whether they reach the gallery.
- Ethical guardrail: secondary links, legal information and image sources remain reachable. Nothing is concealed to force the preferred route.

## Mental model: concrete preview reduces uncertainty

**PLFS: +14** (4 + 5 + 3 + 5 − 3 = 14).

- Why: a real interface helps the visitor form an accurate mental model.
- Behavior: visitor understands what they will install and what the size-first workflow means.
- Where: hero, gallery, captions and feature detail.
- Implementation: actual welcome-screen captures; language/theme captions; honest explanation that other screens are not yet shown. No simulated success treated as proof.
- Test: check whether visitors can distinguish actual captures from the labelled illustrative size control.
- Ethical guardrail: no invented reduction figures, ratings, testimonials or future screens presented as shipped.

## Mental model: transparent risk framing

**PLFS: +15** (4 + 5 + 4 + 5 − 2 = 16, capped).

- Why: explicit limits answer reasonable anxiety without pressure.
- Behavior: visitor makes an informed choice about local processing and quality trade-offs.
- Where: feature copy, FAQ and launch page.
- Implementation: explain re-encoding, device/target limits, local compression versus sharing/network behavior, and the absent Play launch date.
- Test: ask whether the visitor expects lossless compression, a guaranteed target, or an immediate Play download. Correct the copy if those misconceptions persist.
- Ethical guardrail: no fear, countdown, hidden opt-in, blanket privacy promise or forced review. The visitor can simply leave.
