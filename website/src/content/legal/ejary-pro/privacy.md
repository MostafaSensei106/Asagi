This is a draft scaffold for a future Ejary Pro privacy policy. It deliberately contains no
claims about how the app collects, uses, stores, or shares information, because those
practices have not been documented and reviewed yet.

If you are looking for a binding policy, this is not one. Do not rely on this page for
compliance, procurement, or app-store submissions.

## 1. Draft status

This document is published only so the site structure can be reviewed before Ejary Pro ships.
It is marked as a draft, kept out of search engines and the sitemap, and excluded from the
public app listings. The dates shown above record when the scaffold was prepared, not when any
policy takes effect.

## 2. What this policy will cover

The final policy is expected to describe, at minimum:

- What data the app accesses on your device and where that processing happens.
- Which network requests the app makes, and to which services.
- What is stored locally, for how long, and how you can delete it.
- What third-party services are involved and what they receive.
- How to contact the developer about privacy questions or data requests.

Those sections will be written from the app's actual behaviour once it is implemented. Until
then, no such statements exist here.
